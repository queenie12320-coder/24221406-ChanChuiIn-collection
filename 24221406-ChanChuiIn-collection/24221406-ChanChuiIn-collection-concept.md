                                                         Loop of Pure Pleasure and Pain 


The heading for my whole collection is Loop of Pure Pleasure and Pain.The subject of the work is diarrhea, a brief bodily reaction that I frequently get due to my sensitive stomach. I never learn my lesson and continue to eat items that irritate my stomach, even though it happens frequently. Every diarrhea episode is unique. During each episode, I take medicine and go to the toilet immediately so  the episode passes quickly and the waste is flushed away. Therefore no excrement is left behind the specific experience disappears.I can never go back to or repeat an episode of diarrhea, including the pain, the smell in the air.This physical reaction keeps repeating, yet it can never be fully captured. 

I present all 20 records side by side rather than in chronological order.The toilet, food, and medicine are only traces and triggers not the main subjects. People can see how often I get diarrhea if they organize them by date. However, they will overlook my main point. I repeatedly bring this reaction upon myself, which shows my obstinacy in eating foods I like even when I am aware of the negative consequences, because I love eating.

I think audio REC‑005 is the most unique one. It sounds like diarrhea.It accurately depicts the physical sound of diarrhea. As text cannot properly express a state of diarrhea and photographs would be too disgusting, I chose to use audio instead. The audio clearly shows how terrible my stomach was at that time.

Only the external parts of each event, such as the appearance of the food, the medicine packaging , or reflections written after the event are captured in the records. The collection will never be able to capture the actual bodily feelings at that precise moment, such as the smell in the air or the acute cramping in the stomach.Once the diarrhea ends, those feelings disappear completely without leaving any physical mark .
